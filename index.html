Stavros Orfanoudakis

Postdoctoral Researcher · Intelligent Electrical Power Grids, TU Delft

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Intelligent Electrical Power Grids group at TU Delft, working on reinforcement learning, graph neural networks, large language models, and physics-informed machine learning for power and energy systems, with a focus on scalable and trustworthy AI for real-world operation. Previously, I was a Research Scholar at ETH Zürich's Power Systems Laboratory and a Visiting Researcher at MIT's Center for Transportation & Logistics.

Research interests
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Physics-Informed ML
  • LLMs & GNNs for Power Systems
  • Energy Transition & Smart Grids
  • Multi-Agent Systems
